Common questions

Quick answers before you speak to an advisor.

How do I book a site visit?

Send an enquiry using the form above or call our sales gallery. An advisor will confirm a time that suits you and can arrange transport from the nearest station.

Are your projects RERA registered?

Yes. Every Aurelian project is registered, and the registration number is displayed on the project page and in your booking documents.

What payment plans are available?

Most projects offer construction-linked and possession-linked plans. Your advisor will walk you through the schedule before you commit to anything.

Can you help with a home loan?

We work with several leading banks that pre-approve our projects, and our team can introduce you to them and help with the paperwork.
