25 Years of Excellence

A builder whose word has held for 25 years

Aurelian Estates began in 2001 with a single 24-home building in Mumbai. We still build the way we did then: our own engineers on site, materials tested before they are used, and prices that don't change after you have signed.

Today our architects, engineers and craftspeople have delivered more than 50 projects, and every one of them is still looked after by us long after handover.
